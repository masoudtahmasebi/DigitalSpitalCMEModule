## P95-01 — the player has no tab row

The complete layout draws the summary **directly under the video** — module
heading, chapter heading, prose — and no tab row at all. There is no
_Zusammenfassung_ tab, no _Lernerfolgskontrolle_ tab and no _CME Punktemeldung_
tab.

The exam is a **row in the Modul Übersicht**, under the modules: a dark padlock
while it is shut, the layout's orange open padlock and orange label once the
server opens it.

That is the better shape, and for the reason P82-03 already gave: the exam
belongs to the course's structure — which is what that column _is_ — rather
than to whichever section a learner happens to be watching. It also removes the
last place where a padlocked control sat beside the thing it was not.

The Punktemeldung needs no tab either: page 11's **CME-Punkte geltend machen**
is how a passed exam reaches it, which is the route that was always there.

### And the row I added yesterday is gone

P93-03 added `Wird nach Modul 3 freigeschaltet 〔🔒 Zur Teilprüfung〕` between the

### One thing the drawing does not have, kept anyway

**Weiter: ‹next section›**, under the video. P78-02 added it for a reported

## P95-02 — I had the palette backwards

The complete layout draws a **finished module as a teal disc with a white tick**
and a **module under way as an orange disc with pause bars**. P94-02 — this
morning — made both orange, from an export in which the finished module is
orange too.

The split is meaningful: teal is the platform's colour and marks what is _done_;
orange is what `tailwind.preset.js` calls _"resume the thing you started"_ and
marks the one row that wants the learner back. All-orange gives a finished course
five things all asking for attention.

A finished **chapter** is a bare orange tick rather than a disc — the disc
belongs to the module, which is the row somebody scans. `StateIcon` takes a
`tone` for that.

### Both buttons, not one

The layout stacks **orange _Lernerfolgskontrolle beginnen_ above outlined
_Fortbildung pausieren_** once the exam opens. P94-02 swapped one for the other.
They are different actions — start the exam, and stop for today — and a learner
who wants the second should not have to give up the first to reach it. And the
pause is **outlined**, not solid: it is the alternative to the thing in the
accent colour, never the thing itself.

## A defect the fixture exposed while doing this

`examRows` first used the exam's own title. The player fixture has an exam on two
modules and **both are titled "Lernerfolgskontrolle"** — which is not an
authoring mistake, it is the obvious thing to type. Two identical rows are two
rows a physician cannot choose between (§9.4), and no amount of authoring
discipline fixes that from inside the widget.

So: one exam keeps the layout's bare word; several are labelled
`‹title› – Modul N`. The module cannot collide.

## P95-04 — the EFN, and why nothing changed

Page 12 reads _"Die 18-stellige EFN finden Sie auf Ihrem Arztausweis"_ and its
placeholder is eighteen characters. The platform validates fifteen.

**This is already answered.** `docs/show-stoppers.md` **S21** raised it on 05.08
from the earlier deck, and closed it on 08.08 against the Landesärztekammern's
own published description: **fifteen is correct and the layout's caption is
wrong.** The hint says fifteen because it has to agree with the validator, or a
physician is told to type a length the field then refuses.

Nothing to build. **One thing for MEDICE:** page 12's caption should be
corrected before it goes in front of a physician.

## Two observations recorded rather than acted on

- **The progress card on page 6 shows no clock.** At 100 % the card reads only
  "Modul 3 von 3", where page 5 has "14:35 / 25:45". That may be a designed
  state or a simplification in one frame; hiding the clock from somebody
  re-watching a finished video is worse than leaving it, so it is left and
  written down.
- **One flaky admin test, under parallel load.** `pnpm verify` failed once with
  _"Unable to find an accessible element … /Neue Fortbildung/"_ and was green on
  a re-run, and the suite passes alone and together. That string is the exact
  symptom §9.8 records for ambient jsdom state leaking between cases. It is not
  reproducible here and is not claimed as fixed — it is named so the next
  occurrence is the second, not the first.
