# P216 — DEP-38, the teal

> The teal color used every where should be this - #007f95

Done for the palette: `brand.600` is `#007f95` and 50/100/500/700/800 are
rebuilt around it rather than left on the old hue, which would have produced a
hero in one teal and its hover state in another. `accent` (the focus ring's
fallback) moved with it.

Two things this ticket surfaced that are worth more than the hex:

**A hardcoded copy of the palette.** `primitives.tsx` drew the progress ring
with `stroke="var(--ds-brand-600, #17788d)"` — the old default written out a
second time, so the ring would have stayed the previous teal after the palette
moved. It reads `currentColor` off a `text-brand-600` class now, so there is one
home for the value.

**Changing the default may change nothing on this deployment, and that is the
part to check first (§9.9).** `--ds-brand-600` and `--ds-brand-700` are written
by `brandingCssVars` from the project's `primaryColor` in Verwaltung →
Erscheinungsbild. Where a customer has set one, **theirs wins and these defaults
are never read.** So for MEDICE the answer may be a value in their project
settings rather than a release. `brand-50`, `100`, `500` and `800` have no
branding input at all and are always the code's.

## Not done: "Tab border should be this - #007f95"

Raised rather than guessed (§7). The tab and its panel share one outline
deliberately — **DEP-28** reported the seam that appears when they do not, and
the fix made both `brand-100`. Setting that outline to `#007f95` is a visible
design change that either
(a) means the _active tab's_ border only, leaving the panel's as it is, or
(b) means the whole outline, which re-opens DEP-28.

Both are defensible from the sentence, they look different, and the screenshots
that would settle it are Jira attachments. Deciding by preference is how P204,
P208 and P209 each became the next report.
