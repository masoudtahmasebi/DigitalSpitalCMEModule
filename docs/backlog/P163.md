# P163 — Every content ticked, 100 % in the header, and no way to the EFN

**Raised by:** the client, 03.09.2026 —
_"I am done with lernerfolgskontrolle, I don't see the button to go the next
screen to enter the EFN details, could you tell me what is missing here?"_ — and
then, with a hypothesis: _"can it be because in this course the evaluation form,
is it mandatory?"_

**Branch** `claude/education-platform-roadmap-3vgrqh`, from `origin/main` at
`5297304`.

## The hypothesis was wrong, and the answer is one screen up

The evaluation cannot hide that button. `completion.ts:61`:

```ts
const COURSE_CONDITIONS: readonly CompletionCondition[] = ["watch", "quiz"];
```

`courseComplete` is those two and nothing else, and `App.tsx:759` gates the
button on exactly it. The evaluation is the **next screen after** the button —
`{ kind: "evaluation", then: "reporting" }` — so an empty evaluation form is a
real problem for the certificate and cannot be the reason the button is absent.

The quiz was passed: 9 of 11, 81 %, 8 required. So `watch` was the false one —
on a screen whose own header said **"100 % der Fortbildung absolviert"** and
whose sidebar ticked every content.

## P163-01 — the third call site, and the one that decides the CME point

P158 taught `fillSamplingGaps` to close the holes a fifteen-second flush leaves.
It was applied at **two** of the three places stored intervals are credited:

| Where                                            | Segments | What it decides                             |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `learning.service.ts:275`, the write path        | credited | the stored percentage, so the content ticks |
| `learning.service.ts:446`, the read path         | credited | the bar and the gap list on the player      |
| `toContentSegments:1039` → `courseWatchCoverage` | **raw**  | **`achievedWatchPercent`, the gate**        |

So a physician who watched every video end to end through a sampling clock was
credited complete on every content, shown 100 % by the rollup, passed the
Lernerfolgskontrolle — and measured at 597 of 600 seconds by the gate, which
floors to **99**, and 99 does not satisfy a `requiredWatchPercent` of 100.

There was no way onward from that screen and nothing on it named a section to go
back to, because by every other measure there was none.

This is CLAUDE.md §4 invariant 6 in the letter: _one rollup path, because two
implementations eventually disagree and disagreeing numbers on a CME record are
a compliance problem._ The write path's own comment says the credit exists "so
the bar, the number and the gap list all come from one union (§4 inv. 6)" —
three of four, missing the one that awards the point.

**Written red first:**

```
FAIL  coverage.test.ts > reaches 100 % for a video watched end to end through a sampling clock
AssertionError: expected 99 to be 100
```

Fixed inside `courseWatchCoverage`, not at the caller: the duration and the
crediting rule then live in one place, and the caller has the segments but not
the length they must be judged against — which is exactly how the omission
happened. Domain suite 810/810, and the guard case still refuses a genuine
four-minute skip at 60 %.

## P163-02 — "Spulen Sie dorthin" over a player that refuses to spool

_"i am playing the video, and the segment is correct, still there is this
message which can be misleading."_ At 2:06 of a 24:46 video:

> Diese Stellen fehlen noch: 2:06–24:46. **Spulen Sie dorthin**, um die
> Wiedergabe zu vervollständigen.

directly above _"Vorspulen ist nicht möglich."_ Two sentences on one screen, one
telling the learner to seek and the other saying seeking is refused.

**P102-01 already found this exact contradiction** and guarded one case —
`covered.length === 0`, a video nobody had opened. Watch one second and it comes
back, because everything ahead of you is uncovered when you are watching
linearly. §9.11: the instance was fixed and the class was not.

A gap is worth naming only when it lies **behind** the furthest point reached —
there the learner skipped or lost something, seeking back is permitted, and the
sentence is an instruction the player can carry out. The tail ahead of that
point is not a gap; it is the rest of the video.

Two tests, both watched red by restoring the old behaviour.

## Still to do, and it is the client's

for a different reason than the one asked about: with zero questions
`evaluationSubmitted` can never become true, so `complete` stays false — no
certificate and no Punktemeldung, _after_ the physician has typed their EFN,
which is the worst place on the platform to be refused. Not a code defect; the
warning already exists and is accurate.
