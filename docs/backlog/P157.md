# P157 — Whole minutes below the furthest point, on the client's decision

S30, decided. Implements the completion rule Masoud asked for twice, in the one
form that does not reopen what `CLAUDE.md` §4 invariant 5 exists to prevent.

Branch `P157-minute-checkpoints`, from `origin/main` at `bf68526` (PR #44
merged). `api.digitalspital.com/health` reported `cc65b7a` / `0.1.405` before
that merge — established first (§9.9).

## The decision

> the minute check is enough, not every second, if someone reaches the minute 2,
> then we are sure they watched the minute 1, if someone reaches minute 33, they
> have watched until minute 32 at least, and we also can use that checkpoint for
> when we reload the page, it starts from the same place, and we already have
> this

## Why it is not the thing invariant 5 forbids

Invariant 5 says watched percentage is the union of intervals, "never the
maximum playback position", and gives its reason exactly: _a max-position
implementation makes any gate trivially skippable by dragging the scrub bar._

That was true when it was written. **P154-01 made it false**: the forward seek
ceiling is now half a second above the watched edge — an order of magnitude
below the smallest forward control, with a test pinning that ratio — so the
playhead passes unwatched content only by playing it. `playerSeekLimit` advances
`reachedSec` on playback and never on a seek, for the same reason. With the
scrub bar unable to move the ceiling, "the furthest point reached" stops being a
client claim and becomes a consequence of playback.

So the rule credits **completed minutes strictly below that point**, and nothing
above it. A learner at 33:20 is credited through 33:00. A learner at 0:47 gains
nothing, because no minute is finished. Everything past the furthest point is
left exactly as reported.

## Why whole minutes

The gaps this exists for are sampling artefacts — a throttled `timeupdate`, a
flush that failed, a boundary between two samples. They are fractions of a
second to a few seconds. A minute absorbs all of them and is still coarse enough
that skipping a minute is skipping.

It is also the granularity the product already speaks in: `resumePosition`
rewinds to the start of the containing minute. `WATCH_CHECKPOINT_SEC` is
asserted equal to `RESUME_GRANULARITY_SEC`, so the two cannot drift (§9.11) —
a learner who resumes at 33:00 is not then told 32:58–33:00 is missing.

## Stored raw, returned credited

`merged` remains the evidence: every interval the client sent, validated against
the wall clock. `credited` is derived from it for the percentage, the status and
the segments handed back. The rule can therefore be withdrawn without having
destroyed the record it was applied to — which matters for a figure that decides
a CME point.

Both paths credit, which surfaced a real inconsistency: `getLesson` returned
`watched_segments` **verbatim** while the percentage beside it came from the
merged union, so the coverage bar and its number could disagree. That is §4
invariant 6, and it is fixed by the same change.
