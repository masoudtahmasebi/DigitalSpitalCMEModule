# P91 — the frontend against the Zeplin exports, screen by screen

**Status:** P91-01, P91-04 and P91-05 fixed — the last two in **P93-03**, after
the client reported the screen again. P91-02 and P91-03 remain open; P91-02 is a
question for the client rather than a defect.
**Raised by:** the client, 18.08.2026 — _"here are the layouts, can you check the
frontend one more time more carefully against the layout?"_

**Method.** The sixteen PNG exports were sliced and read one by one, and the
same screens were driven in a browser against the local rig and screenshotted at
1440 px and 390 px (`apps/e2e/tests/layout-shots.spec.ts`, temporary). Every
line below is a comparison of two pictures, not a reading of the CSS — which is
the only way this check could have found P91-01, because the markup says what
was intended and the browser says what happened.

**The headline: the frontend matches the drawings closely.** Zertifizierung is
section-for-section identical; the Mediathek's module filter, group headings,
two-column grid and lock overlay are all there; the mobile arrangements — hero
band, meta card, tab buttons below the panel, the pagination's stacked numbers,
the "Weitere" full-width button, the sticky progress teardrop — are all as
drawn. What follows is the short list of what is not.

## P91-01 — the CME seal is cut in half — **fixed**

The catalogue hero draws the seal clipped into its top-right corner: about a
third of it visible, the rest outside the rounded corner.

Two causes, and the first is the interesting one:

1. The positioning box was `sm:inset-0 sm:bottom-auto sm:left-auto`. That reads
   as "from `sm`, cover the hero", and it does the opposite: `bottom:auto;
left:auto` shrink-wraps the box to its content, so the `h-full` inside it
   measured **the seal** rather than the hero and `top-[44%]` centred the seal
   on itself — pinning it to the top-right corner. The four sides are now
   written out (`sm:bottom-0 sm:left-0 sm:right-0 sm:top-0`), which is also why
   the original avoided them: Tailwind emits `inset-0` before `bottom-10`, so a
   bare `sm:inset-0` loses to the mobile anchor.
2. `sm:translate-x-1/2` put half the seal outside the hero on purpose — the
   drawing centres it **on** the content column's right edge, which works there
   because the hero bleeds to the viewport. This hero cannot bleed: the widget
   is as wide as the container its host gives it, so the overhang fell outside
   and `sm:overflow-hidden` cut it.

Now `sm:right-6 sm:top-1/2 sm:-translate-y-1/2`: fully inside the hero, near
the right edge, vertically centred. The mobile anchor — inside the photograph's
bottom-left corner — is unchanged and was always correct.

## P91-02 — the catalogue's tabs: three in the drawing, two in the product

`Uebersicht-V2` draws **On Demand · Live · Präsenz**. We ship **On Demand ·
Weitere**, and `packages/copy/src/de.ts` says so in as many words:

> _Two, matching the layout … `Weitere` covers every delivery type that is not
> on-demand rather than being a `Live` tab and a `Präsenz` tab that both stand
> empty._

That comment is not wrong, and this is the part worth stating: **the two
drawings disagree with each other.** `Uebersicht-Mobile` shows exactly one
button under the panel, labelled **Weitere** — which is what our two-section
model produces, and is presumably what the comment was written against.

So this is a question rather than a defect:

- **Three tabs** matches the desktop drawing and gives Live/Präsenz a home
  before either exists — at the cost of two tabs that say "keine Fortbildungen"
  for as long as MEDICE runs one on-demand course.
- **Two tabs** is what ships, matches the mobile drawing, and needs no change
  when a live event does appear.

Recommendation: leave it until there is a live event to put in it, and revisit
with the client then. Changing it is under an hour either way.

## P91-03 — "Zurück zur Übersicht" is in the wrong place, in the portal only

The drawing puts a plain **← Zurück zur Übersicht** link under the meta bar.
`CourseHeader` draws exactly that — but only when the widget owns catalogue
navigation (`courseSlug === ""`, which is the WordPress embed). In the portal
the host pins the course, so the widget hides its link and `apps/portal` draws
a **bordered secondary button above the hero** instead.

Two controls for one idea, and the one the client sees is the one that does not
match the drawing.

The fix is a host callback, mirroring the one that already exists: picking a
course dispatches a cancelable `ds-lms:course-open` the host may preventDefault
and handle. Going back needs the same shape — a cancelable
`ds-lms:catalogue-open`, plus an attribute so the widget knows a host is there
to handle it and does not draw a control that would do nothing (§9.2). Then the
portal drops its button.

~2 h, and it touches the element's public surface, which is why it is not in
this commit.

## P91-04 — the player never says _when_ the exam opens

`Player-Ansicht-Tab-Zusammenfassung-V2` draws, right-aligned between the video
and the tabs:

> Wird nach Modul 3 freigeschaltet 〔🔒 Zur Teilprüfung〕

We draw nothing there. The padlocked **Lernerfolgskontrolle** tab is present and
announces itself as _gesperrt_, so the exam is not invisible — but nothing on
the screen says what unlocks it, which is the §9.4 half.

**And there is a vocabulary question underneath it.** The same drawing carries
both a _Lernerfolgskontrolle_ tab and a _Zur Teilprüfung_ button. Since P87 a
module may have its own exam, so these could be one thing under two names, or
the module's Teilprüfung beside the course's final Lernerfolgskontrolle. CLAUDE
.md §5 makes the layout's copy authoritative and §7 refuses to guess at which:
**this needs the client's answer before either word ships.**

## P91-05 — the player's furniture is arranged differently

Three placements differ from `Player-Ansicht-*`:

| Drawing                                                                    | Product                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| progress card in the teal header, beside the title                         | inside the white panel, above the video        |
| primary action (**Fortbildung starten / pausieren**) under the module list | under the video, beside "Zurück zur Übersicht" |
| module rows carry no counters                                              | "0/2", "0/3" beside each module                |

Everything the drawing shows is present and correct — module list with locks,
expanded current module with its chapters, the check/pause/play markers, the
"14:35 / 25:45" position, "63 % absolviert", the automatic-saving note. It is
the arrangement that differs, and the differences are not accidents of CSS: the
progress card sits where it does because P76-03's unsatisfiable-gate warning
belongs beside the number it explains.

Worth doing, worth doing deliberately: ~3 h, and it should be one pass with
P91-04 rather than two edits to the same region.

## Deliberate deviations, already decided — for the record

- **The Lernerfolgskontrolle and CME-Punktemeldung tabs are hidden on a module
  that has no exam** (P82-03). The drawing always draws all three, padlocked.
  The client asked for the change: _"if a module does not have erfolgs…"_ — a
  padlock that can never open is worse than no tab.
- **Two lines the drawing does not have**: "0 % der Videoinhalte angesehen
  (erforderlich: 100 %)" on the progress card, and "Diese Stellen fehlen noch:
  …" under the player. Both came from real reports (P76-03, P82-05) where a
  learner could not tell why a gate would not open.
- **The Mediathek's lock overlay repeats per module group** rather than once
  over the whole blurred area. In a group with an odd number of cards the
  sentence lands on top of one — worth tidying, no information lost.

## Not a code finding: the demo tenant has no artwork

Both hero variants render flat teal because `ds` seeds no Titelbild and no
`catalogSealImageUrl`. The drawings' photograph, the split hero and the
customer's own seal are all supported and all come from branding — so the
screens above understate how close the match is on a tenant with images.
