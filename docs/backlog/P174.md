# P174 — The gating thresholds come from the course

**Raised by:** the client, 03.09.2026, answering the open decision P171 left:

> _"The three gating thresholds — required_watch_percent, pass_threshold_percent,
> max_quiz_attempts should come from the course."_

**Branch** `claude/education-platform-roadmap-3vgrqh`, from `ce430fe`.

---

## P174-01 — Live, not snapshotted

`findEnrolment` and `listEnrolments` read all three from `courses`. The
enrolment's own columns are still written at enrolment and are untouched: they
remain the record of what was in force, and are no longer what any decision is
made on. Same shape as P171-01 for points and category, and it completes that
sweep — nothing tenant-visible now decides a gate from a stale copy.

### What it fixes

A screen that disagreed with itself. The Zertifizierung tab renders the
**course's** numbers — "Mindestens 100 % aller Videomodule müssen angesehen
werden", "Mindestens 70 % der Fragen müssen korrekt beantwortet werden" — and
the progress card rendered the **enrolment's**. The moment an operator edited a
published course, a physician was shown two different rules for the one thing
they were being held to, and the gate obeyed the one that was not on the
accreditation panel.

### What it costs, said plainly

These decide whether somebody may proceed, so reading them live is retroactive
by construction:

- raising `required_watch_percent` puts an in-progress learner back below the
  line on videos they have already watched;
- raising `pass_threshold_percent` un-passes a score that cleared the old mark —
  `hasPassedQuiz` compares the stored best against the threshold, so 80 stops
  being a pass when the mark moves to 90, and P170-01's refusal correspondingly
  lets that exam be sat again.

That is the operator's decision to make about work still in progress, and it is
what was asked for.

## P174-02 — What it must not do, and cannot

**Take away something already finished.** `alreadyCompleted` (P167-01) held a
completed enrolment complete against a new condition; it is now fed by
**either** completion timestamp rather than by certification alone:

```
completedAt !== null || courseCompletedAt !== null
```

Without the widening, a physician who finished the Fortbildung last week and has
not yet claimed their point — `courseCompletedAt` set, `completedAt` still null
— would have been un-finished by an operator raising the bar while they were
away. With it, a later edit decides what is still in progress and does not
reopen what is not.

Applied in both readers, so the console and the widget cannot disagree about who
has finished: `learning.service.ts` and `admin.service.ts`.

## The comments this made false

Two, both corrected rather than left (§11.9):

- `enrol`'s header: _"The course's settings are copied onto the enrolment here
  and never read live again (P3-01): a learner who starts under a 70 % threshold
  finishes under it, even if an admin edits the course mid-course."_
- the same claim, restated inside the validity-window branch.

And one **test** that argued the opposite ninety minutes before this ticket:
`measures against the enrolment's threshold, not the course's current one` said
_"a course later tightened to 90 % must not reopen an exam somebody passed at 80
under the rule they enrolled on"_. Its fixture — course 90, enrolment 70 — is a
state production can no longer produce. It is rewritten to pin what the service
is actually responsible for (it judges against the threshold it is **given**,
from one reader), with the retroactive edge named in it, rather than deleted.
