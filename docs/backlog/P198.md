# P198 — A whole video is credited at every speed the player offers

**Raised by:** the client, 07.09.2026, after P196 — _"please make sure that the
user is able to go to next step, no matter what the speed of the video is."_

---

## What P196 left

P196 fixed the mechanism and proved it with a simulation I ran once in a
scratch file. **A simulation run once is a claim, not a check** (§11 rule 3):
nothing in the repository would have noticed if the next change broke it, and
the client had already been told the same thing nine times.

## The check

`watch.test.ts` drives a fifteen-minute video start to finish, flushing on the
widget's own heartbeat, against a server clock that jitters — over
`PLAYBACK_RATES` **itself**, not a list of numbers copied out of it. Adding a 3×
entry to the speed menu fails here rather than costing somebody a CME point,
which is the guard P153-01 established for the same list.

It asserts two things per rate: that the credit reaches 100 %, and that it never
**stalls** — a run where the edge stops advancing while the learner keeps
watching is the defect, and a percentage alone would not name it.

## What it found

The 2 s-of-error case passed. **Four seconds of error did not.** So the guarantee
the client asked for did not hold at the fastest rate, and the number to fix it
had to be chosen rather than guessed:

```
error 2 s -> needs CLOCK_SLACK >= 0.077   (effective cap 2.15x)
error 3 s -> needs CLOCK_SLACK >= 0.167   (effective cap 2.33x)
error 4 s -> needs CLOCK_SLACK >= 0.273   (effective cap 2.55x)
error 5 s -> needs CLOCK_SLACK >= 0.400   (effective cap 2.80x)
```

`CLOCK_SLACK` is **0.3**, which carries four seconds of error on a
fifteen-second heartbeat at 2× with margin.

## What that costs, plainly

The effective ceiling is now **2.6 media seconds per real second** rather than 2.
A client claiming as fast as the gate allows finishes a course in about
three-quarters of the time the fastest honest learner takes.

That is a margin on a rate limit, not a way past the gate. A 952 s video still
cannot be credited without roughly six minutes of elapsed time, and the seek
ceiling still refuses the far side of any hole that has not been reported
across. Both ends are pinned in `watch.test.ts`.

**This number should come back down**, and the way to bring it down is not a
smaller constant. See below.

## The fix this stands in for

The slack exists because a learner is charged **twice** for seconds the budget
itself clamped away: the shortfall becomes a gap between the credited edge and
the player's position, and the gap is charged to the next report as if it were a
hop. That is what compounds, and what the slack is absorbing.

The durable answer is to store the furthest **validated** position beside the
credited union — server-derived, never the client's own `lastPositionSec`, which
is client-supplied and would be a way to declare a ceiling. A gap that lies
behind that mark is content the server already saw and refused; charging it
again is the bug. With it, `CLOCK_SLACK` goes back towards zero.

That is a column, a migration and its own ticket. Named here rather than left as
a number nobody can explain.

## Also fixed here

`applies the wall-clock budget across the whole batch` held `30`, `30` and `50`
as literals. Both moved when the bound moved — twice — and each time the case
stopped testing anything while staying green. It is sized from
`wallClockBudget` now: two segments of two-thirds of the budget each are
individually payable and together are not, whatever the budget becomes.
