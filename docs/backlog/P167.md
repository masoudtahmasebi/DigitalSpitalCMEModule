# P167 — A text section is part of the Fortbildung, and says so

**Raised by:** the client, 03.09.2026, answering `docs/show-stoppers.md` §S33
with the rule **and** its mechanism —

> _"maybe one course is just texts, we should have a frontend checkbox that says
> i have read the text, and then the next button which is disabled becomes
> enabled and that counts as that part as done."_

**Branch** `claude/education-platform-roadmap-3vgrqh`, from `origin/main` at
`4943541`.

## What was wrong

`isCourseComplete` asked about watch coverage and quizzes. `courseWatchCoverage`
counts `kind === "video"`. So a section of prose sat in the **denominator of the
percentage a physician reads** — `isComplianceContent` excludes only `material`
— and in **neither side of the gate**.

Two consequences, both reported from the running system in P166:

- a course of two texts, one video and one exam completed with both texts never
  opened, showing "50 %" beside "Sie haben die Fortbildung abgeschlossen";
- a course of nothing but text completed the moment somebody enrolled.

And there was no mechanism to fix it with: `POST /contents/{id}/progress` accepts
video only, so a text section could not be marked done even if the rule had said
it must be.

## Why it went to the client first

It decides whether CME points can be awarded to somebody who never opened a
section the Anerkennungsbescheid lists. CLAUDE.md §7 — an invented accreditation
rule that ships is worse than a delay. The cheaper reading was available and
would have been wrong if the answer was the other one.

## What it is now

**`reading`**, a completion condition beside `watch` and `quiz`. Every text and
details section acknowledged, or the course is not finished.

**`POST /courses/{slug}/contents/{contentId}/acknowledgement`** is the event.
No body — the act is the whole message. It is an **attestation, not a
measurement**, and neither the contract nor the code pretends otherwise: what it
buys is that a physician cannot be certified for a section they never opened.

Refused for a video or a quiz with 422 — both have completion events of their
own, and accepting an attestation for a video would be a way past the watch
gate. Gated and expiry rules are enforced here exactly as they are for playback,
because a screen is not where a compliance rule lives.

Idempotent, because a double-clicked checkbox is not a different fact.

**The checkbox** says what ticking it _does_ rather than that it happened —
_"Erst dann zählt der Abschnitt als abgeschlossen und es geht weiter."_ A box
labelled only "Gelesen" leaves a physician guessing whether it is a note to
themselves or part of their accreditation (§9.4). Once recorded it stops being a
control and says so, because an input that cannot change anything is not an
input (§9.2).

**Weiter is disabled until it is ticked**, for prose only. Not a second rule —
the server refuses the completion either way — but the screen agreeing with the
server at the point the person acts, rather than letting them walk past a
condition and meet it at the end. A video's way onward is its own completion,
and a disabled Weiter there would contradict the watch percentage beside it.

## The half that stops this being retroactive

`alreadyCompleted` short-circuits the **course** conditions. A physician who
finished under the old gate holds a Teilnahmebescheinigung and may have a
Punktemeldung filed against their EFN; a condition added afterwards must not
reopen that. Same principle as the enrolment's snapshot columns, for a condition
that has no snapshot because it did not exist when they enrolled — and it covers
the ordinary case of an author adding a section to a published course somebody
has already finished.

The admin console applies the identical rule, from the same function, so the
console and the widget cannot disagree about who has finished.

## Verification

**Written red first, in three places.**

- Domain: `reading` outstanding, a text-only course completing once
  acknowledged, an already-completed enrolment staying complete, and the order
  conditions are reported in.
- API, against real Postgres, on **its own course** so nothing can be satisfied
  by the shared fixture's videos: not complete on enrolment; 409 while unread;
  one of two acknowledged is not enough; complete when both are; idempotent; and
  **422 for a video**.
- Widget: the checkbox present, Weiter disabled, Weiter released once the server
  has recorded it, and a video's Weiter untouched.

**Watched red.** Making `hasAcknowledgedReading` always true fails two
integration cases. Removing the `disabled` binding, or the checkbox's own label,
fails the widget case.

Fifteen domain cases predated the condition and were **migrated, not deleted** —
their fixtures now say `readingAcknowledged: true`, which is the state they were
always written in, said out loud now that it has a name.

`pnpm verify` exit 0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A text section can be marked read, and only by the learner reading it
- [x] The course waits for every text section
- [x] Weiter is disabled until the box is ticked
- [x] An already-completed enrolment is never reopened
- [x] A video cannot be completed by attestation
- [ ] Confirmed in a browser against the installation — the client's, after deploy

## Not done

**No browser test.** `apps/e2e` needs a Docker daemon this sandbox does not
have, so the journey does not yet walk a text section. It is the obvious
addition and it is not here.

**Nothing migrates existing enrolments.** An in-progress enrolment on a course
with text sections will now show `reading` outstanding until the learner ticks
the boxes, which is the intended behaviour; a _completed_ one is untouched. No
data migration is needed and none is written.

**P166-04 and P166-05 are still open** — `ended` as evidence a video finished,
and a certified course being indistinguishable in the catalogue.
