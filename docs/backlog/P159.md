## The finding

`docs/design/screens/page-02.png` and `docs/design/mobile/progress-sticky-module.png`
were measured at pixel level rather than eyeballed. **Nothing in the MEDICE
layout is a symmetric rounded rectangle.** Every teal and orange block has three
corners at one radius and a fourth at another, and which corner is odd is
consistent per family — the white cards sweep their bottom-right, the teal
blocks square their top-right. The table of measurements is in
`docs/design/README.md`, "The one-odd-corner shape", and the same table is
restated in the header of `apps/widget/src/components/primitives.tsx` where the
classes live.

The desktop numbers are CSS pixels rather than a ratio, because P158 established
by glyph-ink measurement an hour earlier that `docs/design/screens/*.png` is 1:1
at 1920. The mobile file is 2×, checked independently against the sticky card's
`w-[17.5rem]` — and the two agree: 20 px for the teal family from either export.

The widget rounded all four corners evenly everywhere. So the same mistake was
visible on the meta bar, on its badge, on all four tabs, on the inline progress
card and on the sticky card — which is why it arrived as three tickets rather
than one, and why fixing three components would have left the other three.

One shape in the layout was already right: the **closed** sticky button,
`rounded-full rounded-tr-none`. It was drawn from the mobile export directly
instead of assembled from the same defaults as everything else, and it is the
only one of the seven nobody reported.

`rounded-full rounded-tr-none` is doing real work rather than being shorthand.
CSS clamps a 9999 px radius so the radii on any one edge fit that edge, which on
a box wider than it is tall lands exactly on half-the-height — the measured
radius — with nobody hard-coding the element's height. The active tab rounds
only one corner, so that clamp would give it the _full_ height instead; there
the radius is written out and the height it came from is named beside it.

The two panel borders are a consequence, not a preference: `TabbedPanel` is
shared, so an active tab outlined in teal standing on a grey-outlined panel
would have drawn the seam the folder-tab shape exists to hide, on the catalogue
as well as on the course detail. The catalogue's tab row is not in any of the
three tickets; it is in the diff because the component is.

## The second half of DEP-29

DEP-29 asks for "no additional border outline". The corner was one cause. The
other is a real bug and was found by reading the cascade rather than the
component:

`styles.css` carries the a11y floor, `.ds-lms-root :focus-visible` — one class
plus one pseudo-class, **(0,2,0)**. A Tailwind utility a component writes for
its own focus state is also (0,2,0), because `focus-visible:outline-white`
compiles to `.focus-visible\:outline-white:focus-visible`. Specificity cannot
separate them, so source order decides — and the floor sat at the **bottom** of
the file, after `@tailwind utilities`. Every component that had styled its own
focus indicator was drawn over: the player surface, its play overlay, the scrub
bar, and the sticky card's heading.

On the sticky card that is a 2 px `--ds-accent` rectangle — dark navy, on teal,
at 2 px offset — and it appears **the moment the card opens**, because
`closeRef.current?.focus()` runs in the open effect rather than waiting for a
keypress. That is a rectangular outline inside the card that the drawing does
not have, which is what the ticket describes.

Fixed by moving the floor above `@tailwind utilities` (so a component that
deliberately restyles its focus state wins the tie, and everything that writes
no focus utility — the overwhelming majority — still gets the floor), and by
giving the sticky heading the same `focus-visible:outline-*` idiom the player
already uses. A bare `outline-none` is (0,1,0) and loses wherever the floor
sits, which is why the heading's old `outline-none focus-visible:ring-2` never
did anything: opting out has to be deliberate enough to name a replacement.

**Not verified:** whether Chrome matches `:focus-visible` for that programmatic

button sees a navy rectangle" is not something a command showed.

## Checks

`scripts/check-focus-ring.mjs`, in `pnpm verify` and in CI. It asserts the two
mechanical facts the paint depends on: the floor precedes `@tailwind utilities`,
and no component carries `outline-none` without a `focus-visible:outline-*`
beside it. Both were shown red before being shown green — the first by moving
the rule back below the directive, the second by restoring the sticky heading's
original class list.

`apps/widget/src/components/primitives.test.tsx` is new: six of its seven
assertions fail on `bf68526`, and the seventh is the DEP-28 evidence below.

## The one thing DEP-28 asks for that is not done

DEP-28's **Expected Behaviour** is:

> Active tab: solid teal background with white text and no visible border.
> Inactive tabs: white/light background with teal border and teal text.

That is the layout inverted. Every design render disagrees — `page-02.png`,
`page-03`, `page-04`, `page-05` and all four `desktop/detailseite-*.png` draw
the active tab **white with teal text**, merged into the panel below it, and the
inactive tabs **filled teal with white text**. `docs/design/README.md` row 2.4
has recorded it that way since the page was written, and the README's own rule
is that the PDF wins.

So the fill is unchanged and the ticket is answered on its **complaint** — "the
tabs are visually indistinguishable" — rather than on its proposed remedy: the
shapes now differ as sharply as the fills, and the active tab merges into its
panel the way a folder tab does. Inverting the two would also break that
merge, which is what makes the row read as tabs rather than as four buttons.

Raised on DEP-28 for the client to settle (CLAUDE.md §7). If MEDICE has issued a
newer drawing than `260729_MEDICECMEFortbildungMS.pdf`, it belongs in
`docs/design/screens/` and this is a five-line change.

## Observed, not fixed

The course-detail panel hard-codes `rounded-tl-none` whatever tab is active, so
when the active tab is not the first one the panel keeps a square top-left
corner under a tab that is nowhere near it. The mediathek render
(`desktop/detailseite-mediathek.png`) rounds it. It is a separate ticket: the
panel is the caller's, `TabbedPanel` does not style it, and giving it the
