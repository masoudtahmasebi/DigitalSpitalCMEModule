# P158 — Why "I watched the whole video" was 98 %

Two defects, both proved from one response body the client captured, and both
fixed. Replaying that exact session against the fix takes it from **98 % to
100 %**.

Branch `P158-tail-and-gaps`, from `origin/main` at `bf49fb8`.

## The evidence

```
rejected: [{ segment: { startSec: 2483.913576, endSec: 2490.282667 },
             reason: "beyond_duration" }]
accepted: 0
watchedPercent: 98
watchedSegments: [ 14 intervals, first {0, 15.147841}, last {2325.541798, 2489.117804} ]
```

Computed from it, not guessed:

```
union 2451.60 s of 2490 s  ->  98.46 %          (the screen said 98)
13 interior gaps, total 37.52 s, largest exactly 5.000 s
tail overshoot past stored duration: 0.282667 s
seconds of real watching thrown away by that overshoot: 6.369 s
```

## P158-01 — a quarter-second overshoot discarded six seconds of watching

`validateSegments` refused any segment whose `endSec` exceeded the stored
duration, **whole**. Browsers do not stop `currentTime` exactly at `duration` —
at `ended` it can be a fraction beyond — and the stored length is an authored
number that need not match the file to the millisecond either.

So the **final segment of every video** met this. The learner watched to the
finish, the last report came back `accepted: 0`, and the run-up went with it.

Now clamped to the duration rather than discarded. Bounded by `TAIL_GRACE_SEC`,
because an overshoot of five hundred seconds is not jitter: the pre-existing
test asserting `[0, 2000]` on a 1500 s video is refused stays green, and it is
right to.

## P158-02 — thirteen holes a sampling clock left behind

`timeupdate` fires about four times a second and every browser throttles it.
That session's union had thirteen interior gaps totalling 37.52 s **after the
video had been watched end to end**, and the screen named three of them and
asked the learner to go back and re-watch seconds they had already seen.

`fillSamplingGaps` closes a hole **only between two watched regions**.

### Why this is not the rule that had to be reverted

S32's first form credited every whole minute below the furthest point reached,
and an existing test caught it at once: a learner drags to 09:55 of a
ten-minute video, the client posts one five-second fragment, and nine unwatched
minutes are credited. The lesson was that the forward seek ceiling constrains
the **player**, not the API — anything that can post a segment can put one
anywhere the wall-clock budget allows.

This bridges between neighbours. A single fragment has no neighbour, so the drag
case is refused **structurally** rather than by a threshold. Nothing is ever
added before the first interval or after the last, so an unwatched beginning
stays unwatched and an unwatched end stays unwatched — which is what the
completion gate reads. A hole wide enough to be content is left alone; to have
one bridged, a client would have to post coverage on both sides of every hole,
at most a minute apart, all the way through, which is watching the video with
extra steps.

### The bound, and the client's ten-second question

> maybe 30s is better, what happens if a video is only 10 seconds long?

Bounded twice, because one bound is wrong at one end of the range: a minute is
generous for a forty-minute Fortbildung and absurd for a ten-second one. So the
limit is `min(60 s, a tenth of the video)`. On a ten-second video a nine-second
hole is content and stays open; a tenth of a second still closes.

## Replayed, end to end

```
before:                       98 %
after P158-01 alone:          98 %      (the tail is credited, the holes remain)
after P158-01 + P158-02:     100 %      credited union [{0, 2490}]
```

Both are needed: the tail alone does not reach a hundred, and the gaps alone
leave the last 0.88 s open.

progress card, `… % der Fortbildung absolviert` and

## What was actually wrong

Not the row. The footer already read "these two, left and right":

```
mt-2 flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-x-4 gap-y-1
```

`flex-wrap` keeps them together while they fit and drops the note onto a second
line when they do not, which is what `flex-wrap` is for. They did not fit, and
they could not have.

Measured, in Chromium, at the shipped sizes (`text-sm`/600 for the label,
`text-xs`/400 for the note), with the real Inter faces from `@fontsource/inter`
and with the fallbacks a browser picks when Inter is not installed:

| Text                                           | Inter  | Arial metrics | DejaVu fallback |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------- | --------------- |
| `100 % der Fortbildung absolviert` (shipped)   | 221 px | 217 px        | 260 px          |
| `100% absolviert` (the drawing's label)        | 110 px | 106 px        | 127 px          |
| `Ihr Fortschritt wird automatisch gespeichert` | 268 px | 249 px        | 284 px          |

The card was `lg:w-[26rem]` — 416 px, a 384 px content box after `p-4`. One line
needs label + `gap-x-4` + note: **505 px in Inter**, 483 in Arial metrics, 560 in
the DejaVu fallback. The box was short by 121, 99 and 176 px respectively — in
every font, at every desktop width, since `lg:w-[26rem]` is not a range. The
report is exactly reproducible and was never a rendering accident.

### Where the 26 rem came from — and a correction (§11.7)

**The first version of this file said the drawing was a 1440 px layout rendered
at 1920, its card 432 CSS px, and `26rem` therefore right. That was wrong, and
the conclusion drawn from it — that the S16 wording and the drawing's card are
geometrically incompatible — is retracted.**

The reading came from one number: the drawing's autosave note is 335 px wide
where `text-xs` renders it at 249, so 12 px × 1.333 = 16 px "explained" it.
Comparing widths across two different typefaces cannot carry that weight, and
Masoud then said what the artwork is: **1:1 for 1920×1080**.

Measured against that, at 1:1, by glyph ink rather than by advance width:

| Element in the drawing  | Ink measured | Rendered by                      |
| ----------------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------- |
| `0% absolviert`         | 95 × 12 px   | Inter 14 px / 600 → **94 × 12**  |
| `Modul 0 von 5`         | 102 × 11 px  | Inter 14 px / 700 → **95 × 11**  |
| `Titel der Fortbildung` | 413 × 40 px  | Inter 40 px / 700 → **393 × 39** |

The label is `text-sm`, at 1:1, exactly as this file's components already render
it. Under the 1.333 reading it would have been a 10.5 px label over an 8.25 px
module line, which no hero card has.

So the drawing's card is **576 px = 36rem**, its content column is **1398 px**
(the white panel measures x 261…1658 at 1920), and `lg:w-[26rem]` was not
matching the drawing at all — it was 160 px narrower than it.

That also settles the S16 question this file previously raised: at the drawn
36rem the footer has 544 px and the shipped sentence needs 505, so **the stated
referent fits the card the client drew**, with 39 px to spare. Nothing has to be
traded, and MEDICE have nothing to decide here.

## P158-01 — The card is the width the drawing draws, at two steps

`apps/widget/src/components/CourseShell.tsx`: `lg:w-[26rem]` →
`lg:w-[32rem] xl:w-[36rem]`, plus `max-w-full`.

`36rem` is the drawing, and it is `max-w-xl`'s own value, so the number is
Tailwind's rather than a ruler's. It applies from `xl` (1280) up, where the row
can spare it. At `lg` (1024) a 36rem card would leave the MEDICE title 312 px,
so the card steps down to `32rem` — `max-w-lg` — where the footer's two halves
keep their row and wrap inside it. Below `lg` the card is full width under the
title, which is what `player-ansicht-abgeschlossen.png` draws for the phone.

`max-w-full` because the column this sits in is WordPress's, not ours: a fixed
576 px inside a narrower one overflows the page rather than the card.

## P158-01a — And the row could never have held both

This is the defect the drawing hides, because the drawing's title is
`Titel der Fortbildung`.

The hero row is `flex flex-wrap`, and both children sized to their content. The
MEDICE title is `Basisseminar 2026 – Diagnostik und Therapie bei Erwachsenen` —
**928 px** at `sm:text-3xl`. The portal serves the widget inside
`mx-auto max-w-6xl p-4 sm:p-6`, so its column is 1104 px and this row is 1072.
928 + 24 + 576 = 1528, and 928 + 24 + 416 = 1368 before this ticket: the row
wrapped, and the card landed **under** the title, left-aligned, at every
viewport the platform is served at — 1024, 1280, 1440, 1600 and 1920 alike.
Verified by rendering the real `CourseShell` at each of them: `beside=false`
everywhere, before.

A flex item only shrinks below its content width if it is told to. So the title
takes `basis-full` below `lg` and `lg:basis-[18rem]` above it: it gives way to
the card and wraps to two or three lines, which is what the drawing shows a long
title doing. The row still breaks — card under title — when a host's column
cannot hold 18rem + gap + card, which is the honest answer for a narrow
WordPress column rather than a squashed title.

## P158-02 — And the row is a row whatever the font says

Width alone is not a fix, and the measurements say why: at the `lg` step the
pair fits in Inter by 7 px and in Arial metrics by 29, and **does not** fit in
the DejaVu fallback (560 px needed, 512 available). The widget also renders with `--ds-font-family` set to
whatever a customer licenses. A layout whose compliance with the design depends
on which fonts are installed on a physician's machine is one that is correct on
the reviewer's screen and wrong on somebody else's.

So `apps/widget/src/components/PlayerProgressCard.tsx` gains `lg:flex-nowrap`
on the footer, and `min-w-0` on the note. The row is then a row at every width
and in every font; where the sentence will not fit beside the label, the note
wraps _inside its own column_ instead of dropping below it. `min-w-0` is what
permits that — a flex item's `min-width` is `auto`, so without it the note
refuses to shrink below its longest line and overflows the card.

Below `lg` the wrap stays: `docs/design/mobile/player-ansicht-abgeschlossen.png`
stacks them too, and at 288 px of content there is no row to have.

The expiry line keeps the wrap in every case. `sessionEnded` is three sentences,
not four words; squeezing it into what is left beside the label is how _your
progress is no longer being saved_ becomes four lines of small red text in a
corner.

The floppy moved from a flex sibling of the sentence to an inline element in
front of it, for the case the paragraph above creates: with the note as a flex
row, a wrapped sentence right-aligns its lines and leaves the icon stranded in
the middle of the card, beside nothing. Inline, it sits at the head of the first
line and the rest flows under it. Visible at 1024 in the screenshots this ticket
was verified from.

## P158-03 — The column, and the video that comes out of it

Masoud, with the artwork's own measurements: the video is **1022 px** at 1920,
the panel **1398**, the progress card **576**. All three match what this ticket
measured off the PNG independently — the first time this file's reading of the
artwork has been checked against the source rather than against itself.

The video is not a width anybody sets. It is the remainder of the panel's row:

```
video = column − p-4 − 2 × sm:p-6 − border − gap-6 − sidebar
```

so it is right only when every other term and the column are. Two were not:

| Term    | Was                              | Is                        |
| ------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| sidebar | `18rem` (288 px)                 | `20rem` (320)             |
| column  | 1104 (`max-w-6xl` in the portal) | 1488 (`max-w-screen-2xl`) |

At the old column the same layout left the video **744 px**. No arrangement
inside the widget could have recovered 344 px it was never given, which is why
the previous round of this ticket could only report the gap.

### Rounded to Tailwind's scale, on Masoud's instruction

The drawing's exact figures ask for a 304 px sidebar and a 1480 px container,
and neither is a step in anybody's scale. Taken to the nearest ones —
`20rem` (320) and `max-w-screen-2xl` (1536) — the row comes out:

```
1536 − 48 (sm:p-6, portal) − 32 (p-4, widget gutter)      = 1456  panel
1456 −  2 (border) − 48 (sm:p-6) − 24 (gap-6) − 320       = 1062  video
```

**1062 / 1456 = 72.9 %** of the panel against the drawing's
1022 / 1398 = **73.1 %**. Four per cent larger than the artwork, in the same
proportion, out of Tailwind's own scale — which is the trade that was asked
for, over holding one pixel figure exactly with a container width nobody could
name.

### And the screen that would have been stretched by it

`apps/widget/src/App.tsx`'s outline screen — the course detail — never declared
a width. It took the host column, which was 1104 and near enough to its own
drawing (`detailseite-uebersicht.png`, panel x 262…1329 = **1068**). Widening
the portal for the player would have stretched it 384 px past its artwork,
silently, on a screen nobody was looking at. It is now
`mx-auto w-full max-w-6xl` — the Tailwind step nearest that drawing and,
not by accident, exactly what the portal's container used to impose on it. Not
a new width; the width it always had, written down now that the container no
longer says it.

## What this does not do

- **`pnpm test:e2e` was not run.** There is no Docker daemon in this sandbox
  (`docker ps` → `dial unix /var/run/docker.sock: no such file or directory`),
  so `global-setup.ts` cannot bring up Postgres or the stack. P158-04's
  assertion is therefore unexecuted here; the property it asserts is the one
  measured above, red before and green after, on the real component.
- **It does not touch type.** Measured against the same 1:1 artwork, the hero
  title is drawn at ~40 px where this screen renders `sm:text-3xl` (30 px), and
  the autosave note is drawn at ~16 px where it renders `text-xs` (12 px). Both
  are real deltas from the drawing and neither is a layout fault; rescaling
  headings across the widget is a fidelity decision, not a responsiveness fix,
  and `CourseList` already runs its own hero at `2.35rem`. Raised here rather
  than absorbed.

  It is also most of the card's remaining 13 px: Masoud's annotation gives the
  card **576 × 113** and this one renders 576 × 100. Putting the note at
  `text-sm` would close it — and would also take the footer from 505 px to
  544 against a 544 px content box, which is a one-line footer with **zero**
  slack in Inter and none at all in anything wider. It is not a change to make
  without deciding what happens when it does not fit.

- **It does not full-bleed the teal.** The drawing runs the masthead to the
  viewport's left edge with the content on the 1398 column inside it; this
  renders it as the column plus the widget's own 16 px gutter on each side
  (`-mx-4`). A widget centred in a host's container cannot reach the page edge
  without the host's help, so it is the portal's change to make, not this
  component's.
