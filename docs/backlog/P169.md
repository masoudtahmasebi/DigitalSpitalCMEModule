## P169-00 — The hole is credited, and the answer to the question

The question was a fair challenge to what P168 shipped, and the honest answer is
in two halves.

**With a player, you cannot** — and as of P168-01 that is true of the record and
not only of the browser. To be credited at 07:50 the record must already reach
07:48, and a report that begins further ahead is refused (`beyond_ceiling`).

**Without a player** — a hand-written `POST` — there is exactly one route left,
and it is the reason P168 refused to credit holes on the strength of the
end being reached: hop forward in steps just inside the tolerance. That route is
now **charged**: P168-01 bills the gap a hop leaves to the same wall-clock
budget the playback is billed to, so walking a video costs the same real time as
watching it, capped at `MAX_PLAYBACK_RATE`. What made crediting the hole unsafe
yesterday was that the hop was free. It is not free any more.

So the ruling is implemented, and it is implemented because the ceiling makes it
sound rather than because it was asked for:

`fillSamplingGaps` bridges **every** interior gap. `SAMPLING_GAP_MAX_SEC` and
its tenth-of-the-video companion are gone — a width answers _is this hole too
wide to be jitter?_, and the question that decides a CME point is _could this
learner have got to the far side of it?_

What is still never credited is unchanged and now carries all the weight at this
layer: only **between** two reported regions. Nothing before the first second
reported, nothing after the last. A drag to 09:55 has no neighbour, earns
nothing, and since P168-01 is refused before it is ever stored.

### The cost, stated rather than buried

Two, both real:

1. **`courseWatchCoverage` no longer distinguishes a hole from watched
   material.** The anti-skip property has moved entirely to the write path.
   `coverage.test.ts` says so at the place somebody would read it, and its two
   surviving guards are the ones that still bite here — nothing outside the
   reported range.
2. **It is retroactive.** A record written before P168-01 can contain a forward
   jump the server did not refuse, and its hole is credited now. That is the
   price of healing the enrolments that carry one today, including the one in
   the report. Bounded in the past rather than open-ended.

Weakening `validateSegments` — dropping `previousSegments` at the call site, or
letting the gap go uncharged — turns `fillSamplingGaps` into "coverage is the
furthest position", which is §4 invariant 5 inverted. Both module headers say
so, and both rules have tests that were watched red.

## P169-01 — A certified enrolment sits no more exams (list item 1)

`submit` checked the course's dates and the attempt counter and nothing else. A
physician holding a Teilnahmebescheinigung, with a Punktemeldung already filed
against their EFN, could sit the Lernerfolgskontrolle again and have the attempt
written to the record that Bescheid rests on.

The pass itself was never at risk — `bestScorePercent` is best-of — and that is
exactly why nothing complained for nine phases. The damage is to the evidence,
not to the outcome: an attempt dated after the certificate is an assessment
record that no longer matches the document issued from it.

- **API:** `conflict` when `enrolment.completedAt !== null`, checked before the
  attempt counter so a refused attempt cannot consume one of a course's
  allowance. Asserted to write nothing — a refusal that still records the
  attempt is the defect with an error message on top.
- **Widget:** the intro screen now has three states rather than two. Certified
  draws no sitting button at all and says why where the button was (§9.2 and
  §9.4 together), and drops "gewertet wird immer Ihr bestes Ergebnis" — true,
  and beside a control that no longer exists it reads as an instruction to go
  and find it. **Zurück** stays, because an intro with no controls is a dead end
  and this screen is reachable from a finished course's sidebar.

`completedAt`, not `quizPassed` and not `courseCompletedAt`: retrying to improve
a score before claiming the point stays allowed, and there is a test for that
half so the refusal cannot quietly widen.

## P169-02 — The VNR on the certificate (list item 2)

**No code change. Fixed in P164-01, and here is why the report may still be
true.**

`certificate.repository.ts` reads `vnr: courses.vnr` — the course's own number,
the one the operator typed — since P164-01, which is in `0.1.435` and live.
`download` **re-renders the PDF on every request** from that query, so a
re-download after the fix carries the corrected number.

`Verified:` `certificate.service.ts` `download` → `findSource` → `render` on
each call; `archiveOnce` is a separate write.

What does **not** self-correct: `archiveOnce` stores the _first_ rendered bytes
as the audit record of "what we issued". A certificate first downloaded before
P164-01 has an archive object carrying the old VNR while every subsequent
download carries the right one. Re-archiving would overwrite an audit record, so
it is not something to do quietly (§7) — flagged here, and it needs a decision
rather than a commit.

`Assumed:` that the report predates the `0.1.435` deploy. Not verified against
the running system — §9.9, a report from a URL is a report about a build — and
the check after this deploy is to download the certificate again and compare the
VNR with the course's.

## P169-04 — What each content type contributes (list item 4)

A question rather than a defect, and the answer was derivable from three
different files and written down in none. It is now a table in
`docs/architecture.md` §7, ahead of the watch-percentage section.

The short version: there are **two** numbers.

- **The item rollup** — every content worth 1, done or not done; sums up through
  chapter, module and course. Drives the tiles, "x von y Modulen", the ring and
  the sequential gate. `video`, `text`, `details` and `quiz` all count.
- **The watch percentage** — seconds of video covered, weighted by duration
  across the course. Videos only; there are no seconds in a text section. This
  is what `requiredWatchPercent` measures.

The course gate is three conditions: watch coverage met, every
Lernerfolgskontrolle passed, every text/details section acknowledged. `material`
is in neither number — a download has no completion event, and including it
would leave its chapter permanently unfinished and lock every later module.

No inconsistency found while writing it down. `details` renders through the same
`TextLesson` as `text`, so it carries the acknowledgement checkbox and cannot
lock a course — checked, because a kind that is required and uncompletable is
the shape that would have been hiding here.
