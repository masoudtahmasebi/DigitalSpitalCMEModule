# P190 — the 01.09.2026 layout, measured rather than eyeballed

**Raised by:** the client, 01.09.2026, with
`260901 MEDICE CME Fortbildung layout.pdf` — twelve pages at **1920 × 2655 pt**
— plus five annotated screenshots carrying explicit measurements.

**Why this is a phase and not a tidy-up:** the pages are recognisable, and the
product had drifted from them in one specific way that nothing on the project
could have caught. Every screen of the CME module is drawn on **one column** —
1398 px wide, x = 261 … 1659 at 1920 px — and the three screens that make up the
module each had a different answer:

| Screen                    | What it did                                               |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Catalogue (`CourseList`)  | its own `max-w-[1082px]`, 316 px too narrow               |
| Course detail (`App.tsx`) | `max-w-6xl` — its **panel's** width imposed on the screen |
| Player / exam / EFN form  | no column at all — the host page's full width             |

That is CLAUDE.md §9.11's shape exactly: not three bugs, one measurement read
three times. On a WordPress page that gives the widget the viewport — which is
how MEDICE embeds it — two of the three screens ran edge to edge at 1920 px.

**No API, schema, gate or compliance rule changed.** Everything here is layout,
copy and one missing affordance.

## How the numbers were obtained

Not from the thumbnails. The PDF is 1920 pt wide, so one point is one CSS pixel:
each page was rendered at `-r 72` and the landmarks read off the bitmap, then
checked against the annotated screenshots, which agree.

| Landmark                                      | Measured              | Annotation    |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ------------- |
| Content column                                | x 261 … 1659 (1398)   | 1398 px       |
| Course-detail meta strip                      | 1397 × 86, x 261      | 1397 / 86     |
| Ihr Fortschritt card                          | 283 × 328, right 1657 | 282.8 / 328.6 |
| Catalogue filter select                       | 642 × 45              | 684 / 45      |
| **Zur Fortbildung**                           | 170 × 44              | 170 / 45      |
| Catalogue card                                | 1320 × 335, image 525 | 338.2         |
| Detail hero, teal half                        | x 0 … 959             | —             |
| Player white panel                            | 1397, video 1015      | —             |
| Player sidebar                                | 313 wide, x 1321      | —             |
| Hero seal, centred on the column's right edge | centre x 1658.5       | —             |

The one disagreement is the filter select: the screenshot annotates 684 px,
which is 1398 halved with no gap. The PDF draws 642 with a ~31 px gap, and 642 is
what a 1398 px panel with 40 px padding and a `gap-8` grid produces. The PDF
wins because the two halves have to add up to something.

## P190-01 — one content column, applied by three screens

`apps/widget/src/layout.ts` is new and holds the measurement: `CONTENT` (the
centred column), `CONTENT_WIDTH`, and `MAIN_ASIDE` — the course detail's
1070 / 43 / 283 split, which `gap-10` + `18rem` reproduces exactly against the
1398 column.

The player keeps DEP-24's own pair (`gap-6` + `20rem`) rather than gaining a
second one. That ticket measured the same sidebar off a different export and
landed on 304 px where this one reads 313; it rounded up to a Tailwind step and
this one would land on the drawing exactly, and the two are 8 px apart. Two
readings of one measurement is what this file exists to prevent, so the
difference is written down in `layout.ts` and not resolved by preferring the
newer number.

### What this corrects in DEP-24

DEP-24 measured `detailseite-uebersicht.png` and found the course detail's tab
panel at x 262…1329 — 1068 px. That is right, and the 01.09.2026 export agrees
to within two pixels. What it then did was cap the **screen** at that figure
with `max-w-6xl`, and the screen is wider than its panel: the _Ihr Fortschritt_
card sits at x 1374…1656 on the same page, entirely to the right of 1329. A cap
at 1152 has to hold both, so the panel came out at roughly 808 px and the card
was squeezed beside it — a correct measurement of the wrong box.

With the screen on the column, the panel's 1068 falls out of `MAIN_ASIDE`
instead of being imposed: 1398 − 40 − 288 = 1070.

### The part that is easy to get wrong

The hero, the course artwork and the player's teal band all **bleed past the
column**, and `-mx-4` does not do that. A negative margin cancels padding; it
cannot cancel centring. The first attempt kept the bands inside the column and
they bled by exactly the 16 px gutter — a full-bleed teal band with 245 px of
page either side of it, on every course screen.

So the bands sit **outside** the column and apply it to their own contents:
`StickyMetaBar` insets its meta strip, `CourseShell` insets both its band's
contents and the white panel below. The course-detail heading gets half a
column, right-aligned in the hero's left half, so it lands on x = 261 with the
teal still running to the edge of the page.

### The check that can go red

`apps/widget/src/layout.test.tsx` renders all three screens and asserts each
carries the column — and that `CourseShell` carries exactly **two**, because one
would mean the band had swallowed the panel or vice versa. Broken on purpose by
deleting the panel's column: `expected 1 to be 2`.

A pure test of `layout.ts` would have passed with every call site deleted (§9.7),
which is the whole reason it names the callers instead.

## P190-02 — the Punktemeldung was a row nobody had drawn

Every player and exam page of the layout carries **CME-Punkte geltend machen**
under the module list: a grey padlock while the exam is outstanding, an orange
open padlock once it is passed, a tick once it is reported.

P95-01 read this off the previous layout and built half of it — the exam row —
and the sentence written for the other half, `player.reportingLocked`
("Wird nach bestandener Lernerfolgskontrolle freigeschaltet"), reached no
screen. `check:copy` had it **baselined as known-dead** since P123-02. That is
§9.3 with a receipt: a string translated, covered by `check:i18n` at 100 %, and
rendered by nothing.

It is drawn now, and the shut state is deliberately **not a button** (§9.2): the
API refuses a completion whose exam is outstanding, and a control that can only
produce a 409 would produce it after a physician had committed to the step. The
callback comes from `Loaded`, gated on the server's own `courseComplete` — the
same guard the passed-quiz screen's claim button already used (P82-01) — and
routes through the Evaluationsbogen when that is still outstanding, because the
API refuses a completion without one.

Four cases in `ModuleSidebar.test.tsx`, including the one that matters: shut, it
says **what opens it**.

## P190-03 — copy the layout settles

| Was                                        | Is                                                        | Why                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Modul Übersicht`                          | **Fortbildungsfortschritt**                               | every player and exam page heads the column this way, and it is the more accurate word: the rows carry state, and the exam and the Punktemeldung are steps in it |
| `Bestehen`                                 | **Bestehensvoraussetzung**                                | as drawn                                                                                                                                                         |
| `Mind. 8 von 11 richtig`                   | **Mind. 8 von 11 Fragen müssen richtig beantwortet sein** | as drawn                                                                                                                                                         |
| `Fortbildungabschluss starten`             | **Prüfung starten**                                       | see below                                                                                                                                                        |
| `Fortbildungabschluss wiederholen`         | **Prüfung wiederholen**                                   | same                                                                                                                                                             |
| `Lernerfolgskontrolle beginnen`            | **Prüfung starten**                                       | the sidebar action, as drawn on page 06                                                                                                                          |
| `Sie haben die Fortbildung abgeschlossen.` | `… erfolgreich abgeschlossen.`                            | as drawn                                                                                                                                                         |

**On the exam buttons, which reverses P87-02.** That ticket put the exam's own
title in the button, and the problem it solved was real: with one
Lernerfolgskontrolle per module, "starten" under a heading saying
"Abschlussprüfung" leaves a physician unable to tell which exam they are sitting
(§9.4). The new layout solves it a page higher and better — the exam's name is
the **eyebrow directly above the heading**, and it is the sidebar row they
clicked to get here, so it is on screen twice before the button is reached. With
the question answered there, the button names the act, as all four exam pages
draw it.

The exam intro also loses its third stat card (`Antwortformat`), because the
drawing has two. `check:copy` immediately failed with four newly-dead strings,
which is the check working: they are deleted. The two captions that said how
many answers a question takes moved under the question count rather than out of
the product — a physician about to sit a mixed exam still has to know.

### One deviation kept, deliberately

`63 % der Fortbildung absolviert`. The layout says a bare `63% absolviert` and
does not say what it is a percentage of; that is **S16**, open, owned by MEDICE,
and inventing a referent is what §7 refuses. Unchanged.

## P190-04 — the player is one object again

The video and its controls were a rounded black box, an 8 px gap, and a toolbar
sitting on the panel's white. The drawing has a single player. They are now one
`overflow-hidden` frame.

**Attached, not overlaid**, which is the other way to read the drawing: an
overlay covers the bottom of the frame, and this player's control row carries a
scrub bar whose four layers are the visible record of what the CME gate has
credited. Its contrast argument (§ `SeekBar`, WCAG 1.4.1) is written against a
light background and does not survive being put over moving video. The skin is
therefore unchanged and the difference is recorded here rather than absorbed.

The player also stops drawing its own **Zurück zur Übersicht**: `CourseShell`
draws that button in orange at the top of every one of these screens and the
drawing has exactly one of it. Two identical back buttons forty pixels apart
were the same way out twice, not a second way out.

## P190-05 — the catalogue card's picture never filled its card

`sm:h-auto` on an `<img>` whose flex parent is stretched: the widget's own reset
carries preflight's `img { height: auto }`, which beat `self-stretch`, so the
picture sized to its intrinsic ratio and a card with a four-line description
drew a strip of card-coloured nothing under its own artwork — at every width
above `sm`, on the first card of the catalogue, since the card was written.

`h-full` plus `object-cover` for the `<img>`; the placeholder `<div>` keeps
`h-auto`, because `height: 100%` against an auto-height flex container collapses
it to the icon inside — which is the 40 px grey strip the first attempt drew.
The picture's share of the card is now the drawing's proportion (39.8 %) rather
than a fixed rem width, so the split survives the column narrowing on a laptop.

Postgres and the full stack, which this environment does not have. Nothing in
this phase touches the API, so the integration suite has no new surface — but
the browser journey does, and it is the suite that would actually measure a
column (§9.13). It has to run before this deploys.

## Still open

- **S34** — the layout draws an **18-digit** EFN and every part of this platform
  takes 15. The field's _shape_ was adopted (a spaced specimen number, §9.4) and
  its length was not, because the digit count of an EFN is the Ärztekammern's
  rule and not a drawing's (§7). Raised rather than guessed.
- The player's control row is light where the drawing is dark — P190-04 says
  why, and it is a decision to confirm rather than an omission.
- The course detail's artwork is drawn slightly taller than the teal beside it
  in the PDF; ours are equal-height, which is what `grid-cols-2` with
  `object-cover` gives and what survives an artwork of any aspect ratio.
