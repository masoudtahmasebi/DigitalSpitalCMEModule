# P126 — The MEDICE course as MEDICE actually specified it

**Status:** P126-01 built. Two things it cannot supply are named at the bottom.

---

## What arrived

A content sheet (`MEDICE_Fortbildung_content.xlsx`) and the exam
(`Lernerfolgskontrolle.docx`), on 31.08.2026, with DEP-22 opened to test against
them.

The seed had been placeholder since it was written: five modules of one chapter,
two invented experts, and eleven questions reading _"Frage N – Platzhalter"_ with
four options each and **the correct answer always first**. A physician who
answered (a) eleven times scored 100 %.

## P126-01 — structure, experts, descriptions, and a real answer key

Three modules of two chapters, one video each, and the Lernerfolgskontrolle as
**Kapitel 3.3** — a chapter of its own at the end of Modul 3. That is what the
sheet lists, and it is also what the gate wants: `contentGates` opens a module's
exam once that module's _videos_ are finished, so an exam sharing a chapter with
the video it examines is the P87-02 shape that read as unlocked from enrolment.

Chapter bodies, the Detailseite description and the two named experts all come
from the sheet verbatim.

### The answer key, and why it is written out

The Word document marks the correct option of each question in **bold** and says
nothing else. Eleven questions, eleven bold options, exactly one per question —
unambiguous, and still an inference from formatting rather than a stated key.

It decides whether a physician passes a CME exam, which §7 puts in the class of
thing not to guess at. So the key is written out in full in the seed where a
person can read it back against the document, and `medice-content.test.ts`
asserts the properties a transcription error actually breaks: eleven questions,
five options each, exactly one correct index and in range, and — the case that
would have caught the old fixture — **not every answer in the same position**.

Both directions were watched to go red.

The arithmetic needed no change: `scoreQuiz` floors the percentage, so 8/11 is
72 % and passes, 7/11 is 63 % and fails. `assessment.test.ts` has asserted that
under the heading "MEDICE configuration" since before this ticket, and it agrees
with the client's own statement of it.

### Two departures from the sheet, both deliberate

**The course title is unchanged.** The sheet heads the Fortbildung _"Basisseminar
2026 – ADHS Akademie adult"_; the Anerkennungsbescheid and `courses.title` say
_"ADHS Akademie adult"_, and that string is printed on the
Teilnahmebescheinigung. A certificate whose title differs from its Bescheid is a
certificate that does not match. Treated as a working label and raised.

**`Diagonstik` is spelled `Diagnostik`.** A typo in the sheet's module heading
that its own chapter titles do not have. §5 makes the layout's copy
authoritative; a misspelling is not copy.

### The exemption that stopped being true

`check-seed-structure` exempted this course, because it "mirrors the real ÄKWL
Anerkennungsbescheid, which has one chapter per module". With two chapters per
module that sentence is false, and the course clears the threshold unaided — so
the exemption is removed rather than reworded. `EXEMPT_COURSES` is now empty,
which is the right state for a list whose only entry was a claim about the world
that the world stopped satisfying. Verified against a real database: the check
now reports `✓ adhs-akademie-adult: 3 modules, 7 chapters`.

## Verified against a database, not only by typecheck

A migrated Postgres was provisioned, the seed run against it, and the result read
back with SQL: the seven chapters in order, the quiz in Kapitel 3.3 alone, and
all eleven questions with five options and exactly one correct each — key
`B D B B B A B A A B B`. That is the artifact to check against the Word document.

## What this cannot supply

**There is no video.** The sheet names six files and ships none; its own comment
column is MEDICE asking whether they or MiM will produce them. The seed still
points at `media.example.org`, a domain nobody owns (P72). **DEP-12 — the video
gating, seeking and resume ticket — cannot be run until real media is attached
through the console**, and it is the ticket that covers the accreditation's watch
condition.

The durations are the same promise, and the P75-01 report is what it costs: a
gate demanding 25 minutes of a 45-second file. They are set short and uniform
rather than plausible-looking so nobody reads them as a specification; the
console rewrites a duration from the file it is given.

**There are no images.** The Zeplin project needs an account and is not reachable
from a sandbox whose egress is an allowlist. Placeholders stay as they are.
