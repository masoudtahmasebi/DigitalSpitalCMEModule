# P156 — The progress card's footer was two lines, and the drawing's card was never measured for the sentence we ship

DEP-24, reported by Amruth on 02.09.2026 against the design: in the player's
progress card, `… % der Fortbildung absolviert` and
`Ihr Fortschritt wird automatisch gespeichert` are stacked, where
`Player-Ansicht-*` draws them on one line — the percentage left, the floppy and
the note right.

Branch `main`, from `origin/main` at `bf68526`.

## What was actually wrong

Not the row. The footer already read "these two, left and right":

```
mt-2 flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-x-4 gap-y-1
```

`flex-wrap` keeps them together while they fit and drops the note onto a second
line when they do not, which is what `flex-wrap` is for. They did not fit, and
they could not have.

Measured, in Chromium, at the shipped sizes (`text-sm`/600 for the label,
`text-xs`/400 for the note), with the real Inter faces from `@fontsource/inter`
and with the fallbacks a browser picks when Inter is not installed:

| Text                                           | Inter  | Arial metrics | DejaVu fallback |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------- | --------------- |
| `100 % der Fortbildung absolviert` (shipped)   | 221 px | 217 px        | 260 px          |
| `100% absolviert` (the drawing's label)        | 110 px | 106 px        | 127 px          |
| `Ihr Fortschritt wird automatisch gespeichert` | 268 px | 249 px        | 284 px          |

The card was `lg:w-[26rem]` — 416 px, a 384 px content box after `p-4`. One line
needs label + `gap-x-4` + note: **505 px in Inter**, 483 in Arial metrics, 560 in
the DejaVu fallback. The box was short by 121, 99 and 176 px respectively — in
every font, at every desktop width, since `lg:w-[26rem]` is not a range. The
report is exactly reproducible and was never a rendering accident.

### Where the 26 rem came from, and why it is not the drawing's fault either

`docs/design/desktop/player-zusammenfassung-v1.png` is a 1920 px render. Scanning
it for the card's own pixels puts it at **x 1082…1657, 576 px wide**, with 23 px
of padding, and the two footer items at 96 px and 335 px. The note at 335 px is
`text-xs` scaled by 1.333 — 12 px × 1.333 = 16 px, and 249 px × 1.333 = 332 px,
which is the 335 measured. So the drawing is a 1440 px layout rendered at 1920,
its card is **432 CSS px**, and `26rem` was matching it to within 16 px.

At 432 px the drawing's own footer needs 110 + 16 + 268 = **394 px** of a 400 px
content box. It fits with six pixels to spare — because the drawing's label is
the bare `0% absolviert`.

The label we ship is S16's. `docs/show-stoppers.md` records why: `63% absolviert`
names no quantity, three different quantities are all plausible at 63 %, and
CLAUDE.md §7 forbids guessing at a number a physician reads as a statement about
whether they earn their points. So the sentence gained `der Fortbildung`, and
with it 111 px that the card the drawing specifies has nowhere to put.

**The client's report and S16 are geometrically incompatible at the drawing's
card width.** That is the finding; the rest is what to do about it.

## P156-01 — The card takes the width the German sentence needs

`apps/widget/src/components/CourseShell.tsx`: `lg:w-[26rem]` → `lg:w-[36rem]`,
plus `max-w-full`.

36 rem is 576 px, a 544 px content box, which holds 505 px (Inter) and 483 px
(Arial metrics) with room left. `max-w-full` because the column this sits in is
WordPress's, not ours: a fixed 576 px inside a narrower one overflows the page
rather than the card.

The card is now 33 % wider than the drawing's 432 px. That is a visible deviation
and it is deliberate — the alternative is to shorten the label back to the
drawing's, which is S16's decision and MEDICE's to make, not this ticket's. It
is written up for them in `docs/show-stoppers.md` §S16.

## P156-02 — And the row is a row whatever the font says

Width alone is not a fix, and the measurements say why: at 576 px the pair fits
in Inter and in Arial metrics and **does not** fit in the DejaVu fallback (560 px
needed, 544 available). The widget also renders with `--ds-font-family` set to
whatever a customer licenses. A layout whose compliance with the design depends
on which fonts are installed on a physician's machine is one that is correct on
the reviewer's screen and wrong on somebody else's.

So `apps/widget/src/components/PlayerProgressCard.tsx` gains `lg:flex-nowrap`
on the footer, and `min-w-0` on the note. The row is then a row at every width
and in every font; where the sentence will not fit beside the label, the note
wraps _inside its own column_ instead of dropping below it. `min-w-0` is what
permits that — a flex item's `min-width` is `auto`, so without it the note
refuses to shrink below its longest line and overflows the card.

Below `lg` the wrap stays: `docs/design/mobile/player-ansicht-abgeschlossen.png`
stacks them too, and at 288 px of content there is no row to have.

The expiry line keeps the wrap in every case. `sessionEnded` is three sentences,
not four words; squeezing it into what is left beside the label is how _your
progress is no longer being saved_ becomes four lines of small red text in a
corner.

## P156-03 — The journey notices it now

`apps/e2e/tests/journey.spec.ts`, at the player: the label and the note share a
row, and the note is to the right of the label.

The property rather than the pixels (§9.13, and `katalog-siegel.spec.ts` for the
same shape) — it survives a reworded sentence, a different font and a different
card width, and is false in exactly the state that was reported. A jsdom
component test cannot hold it: jsdom does no layout and every
`getBoundingClientRect` there is zero, which is why nine phases of widget tests
were green on a card the client could see was wrong.

## Verification

`Verified:` the numbers above and both states below were measured in the
Chromium the image ships (`/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194`), rendering the real
`PlayerProgressCard` through `react-dom/server` under the widget's own compiled
Tailwind (`npx tailwindcss -c apps/widget/tailwind.config.js -i src/styles.css`),
inside a `.ds-lms-root`, with the real Inter faces registered.

Before, viewport 1440, card 416 px (the shipped width):

```
autosave | card 416 | Inter     | STACKED  | left 221x20 | right 267x16 | rightOf=false
autosave | card 576 | system-ui | STACKED  | left 260x20 | right 284x16 | rightOf=false
```

After, same page, same browser:

```
autosave | card 576 | Inter     | SAME ROW | left 221x20 | right 267x16 | rightOf=true
autosave | card 416 | Inter     | SAME ROW | left 165x40 | right 201x32 | rightOf=true
autosave | card 576 | system-ui | SAME ROW | left 252x40 | right 274x32 | rightOf=true
expired  | card 576 | Inter     | STACKED  | left 221x20 | right 542x32 | rightOf=false
```

and at a viewport of 800 px, below `lg`, the phone arrangement is unchanged:

```
autosave | card 288 | Inter     | STACKED  | left 221x20 | right 267x16 | rightOf=false
```

`Assumed:` nothing about the installation. §9.9 applies as usual — this is a
widget change, so it is live only where the bundle is deployed.

## What this does not do

- **`pnpm test:e2e` was not run.** There is no Docker daemon in this sandbox
  (`docker ps` → `dial unix /var/run/docker.sock: no such file or directory`),
  so `global-setup.ts` cannot bring up Postgres or the stack. P156-03's
  assertion is therefore unexecuted here; the property it asserts is the one
  measured above, red before and green after, on the real component.
- **It does not re-open S16.** The label still reads
  `… % der Fortbildung absolviert`. If MEDICE would rather have the drawing's
  card back than the drawing's sentence, that is one line in
  `packages/copy/src/de.ts` and this ticket's width change reverted with it.
