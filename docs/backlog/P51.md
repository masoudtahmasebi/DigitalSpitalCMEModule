# P51 — The course is finished before the paperwork is

Raised by the client on 11.08, answering the three questions left open by P50:

> the course is complete, they can add `Evaluationsbogen and an EFN` later, we
> mark their progress is pending. we can keep the existing and do not let them
> go on.
> watch gate percentage can be changed from the admin panel.

Three answers, two of which were changes and one of which was already built.

---

## P51-01 · Split course completion from CME certification · 4 h

**Status: done.**

### The problem

`completed_at` meant four things at once: watched, passed, evaluated, EFN on
file. A physician who had watched every minute and passed the
Lernerfolgskontrolle was told the course was **incomplete**, because they had
not yet filled in an evaluation form. The Übersicht showed no acknowledgement
of any kind — just a form.

That is untrue as a statement about the Fortbildung, and it is untrue at the
exact moment somebody has just spent an hour earning the right to be told
otherwise.

### The change

Two milestones instead of one.

| Milestone           | Conditions                                 | What it gates                           |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| **Course complete** | watch threshold, Lernerfolgskontrolle      | nothing — it is a statement, not a gate |
| **Certified**       | + Evaluationsbogen, + EFN where accredited | the certificate, the EIV Punktemeldung  |

`isCourseComplete` in `@ds/domain` now returns `courseComplete` alongside
`complete`, and `outstandingForCourse` alongside `outstanding`. **`complete`
keeps its meaning exactly** — every existing caller of it gates something that
must not happen without an EFN, and redefining the field they already read
would have loosened all of them at once. The new field is the weaker one, which
is the direction that cannot turn a missing EFN into a filed Punktemeldung.

`enrolments.course_completed_at` (migration `0037`) records when the course was
finished. Deliberately **not backfilled**: a row certified under the old rule
was complete, but we do not know _when_ it became complete, and an invented
date on a compliance record is worse than an absent one.

### Acceptance criteria

- [x] Watching + passing reports `courseComplete: true` with `complete: false`
      and `outstanding: ["evaluation"]`
- [x] `complete` still requires the evaluation and the EFN — the completion
      endpoint refuses without them, unchanged
- [x] A property test over all sixteen condition combinations pins
      `courseComplete` to the watch and the quiz **and to nothing else**
- [x] A property test pins the implication: certified ⇒ course complete, never
      the reverse
- [x] The Übersicht says _Fortbildung abgeschlossen_ as soon as the course is
      done, and names where to go next while certification is outstanding
- [x] The console's participant list separates the two, with a
      **Zertifizierung offen** filter — the list of people worth chasing
- [x] The CSV export carries both milestones and both dates
- [x] `contracts/openapi.yaml` describes both, SDK regenerated, `check:sdk` green

### Two things found by breaking the rule to watch the tests fail (§9.1)

**A latent 500 on the completion path.** `complete()` recomputes the state
before it writes, and that recomputation stamps `course_completed_at` from a
clock read _later_ than the `completed_at` taken at the edge. For any learner
who certified without re-reading their state in between — and for every
enrolment predating migration `0037` — the row violated
`enrolments_course_completed_before_completed` and the whole completion failed
at its final step. Fixed by clamping in `markCompleted`: the course was
finished no later than it was certified, which is true by definition.

This did not turn up in normal runs because the ordinary click path reads state
between the two. It turned up the moment the rule was disabled to check the
tests could go red, which pushed every certification down the uncommon path.

**Two tests that passed on the broken system** (§9.7), both mine, both fixed:

- `refuses completion` asserted only `409` — but completion on that course was
  already a 409 for the missing evaluation, so it stayed green with the window
  check removed. Now asserts the _reason_, which is what the learner acts on.
- `writes nothing when it refuses the playback` compared the watch percentage
  before and after against a video that was already 100 % watched. Both
  readings were 100 either way. Now clears the progress first.

## P51-02 · An expired course keeps the record and stops the progress · 2 h

**Status: done.**

P50-01 refused _new_ enrolments outside the validity window and deliberately
left existing learners alone. The client's answer closes that: keep the
enrolment, do not let them continue.

**Kept:** the enrolment, the stored progress, the quiz score, the certificate if
one was earned, and read access to all of it. `GET /enrolment` still answers 200. Losing the record would be a different and worse thing than freezing it.

**Refused:** every write that advances the course — watch segments, quiz
attempts, the evaluation, and completion. `409`, not `404`: the learner is
enrolled and looking at the course, so the thing that changed is the world, not
their permissions, and a 404 here would also be a worse answer than the one
`enrol` gives a stranger.

`PUT /profile/efn` is **not** refused. The EFN is a property of the physician,
not of a course, and they may well need it for a different one.

The German copy says the results are kept, because the first thing anybody
thinks when a button they have used for an hour stops working is that they have
lost the hour.

### Acceptance criteria

- [x] Playback, quiz attempts, the evaluation and completion are refused on an
      expired course, each with its own test naming the call site
- [x] A refused write leaves the stored progress untouched — asserted from a
      cleared state, so the assertion can fail
- [x] Reading the enrolment still succeeds
- [x] The refusal names the expiry rather than the outstanding conditions
- [x] All five refusals verified red with the check disabled

### The cost, stated rather than softened

A physician who finished the course inside the window and returns for the
paperwork after it closes **cannot claim the point they earned**, and no
operator tool exists to grant it. That follows from the instruction as given
and it is raised as **S28** rather than quietly softened — it is an
accreditation question, and CLAUDE.md §7 says not to invent answers to those.

If the Kammer dates participation by the course content, the fix is one
conditional in `CompletionService.complete`, keyed on `course_completed_at`.
The date is recorded for that reason.

## P51-03 · The watch percentage in the console · 0 h

**Already built — no change.** `apps/admin/src/components/CourseSettings.tsx`
edits `requiredWatchPercent` per course, under _Compliance_, beside the pass
threshold, with a warning that changes are not retroactive.

They are not retroactive because the enrolment snapshots the threshold when the
learner enrols (P3-01): a physician who started under 80 % finishes under 80 %,
whatever the course says later. That is the correct behaviour for a compliance
setting and the notice on the screen says so.

**Note for the ADHS course specifically:** the Anerkennungsbescheid makes 70 %
correct answers a _condition of the accreditation_. That is the quiz threshold,
not the watch threshold — but the same screen edits both, and lowering the quiz
threshold on that course voids its accreditation. Recorded on P2-02 and P9-03.

## Still open from P50

**The watch gate has no wall-clock floor.** A single POST claiming 900 seconds
of playback the instant after enrolling is still accepted; `validateSegments`
only rejects segments claiming more playback than elapsed _between_ reports, so
a client that reports once is not caught. Unchanged, because tightening it
would reject a legitimate client that buffers a whole chapter before reporting,
and the client has not said how hard the gate is meant to be.
