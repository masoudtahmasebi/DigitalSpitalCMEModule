## P243-01 · A warning before a course stops being offered, and the day it stops

### Context

`courseAvailability` returns `ended` the instant after `valid_to`, and from then
on the catalogue hides the course, `enrol` refuses new learners and
`requireCourseStillOffered` refuses every enrolled physician's next step
(learning, assessment, completion). That is correct. What was missing is anyone
being told first: grep for an expiry signal in `apps/` and `packages/` found
nothing (S14, §9.10).

Building it found the day itself was wrong in both writers:

| Writer  | Stored for "bis 12.10.2026" | In Berlin        | Effect                                                         |
| ------- | --------------------------- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| console | `2026-10-12T00:00:00.000Z`  | 12.10. **02:00** | course closes ~22 h early, on the last day the Bescheid covers |
| seeds   | `2026-10-12T23:59:59Z`      | **13.10.** 01:59 | Zertifizierung tab reads "bis 13.10.2026"; open 2 h too long   |

Verified: `CoursePresentation.tsx` `toInstant` returned `${value}T00:00:00.000Z`;
`admin.service.ts:225` stores the instant unchanged; `medice-adhs.ts:913` and
`ds-demo.ts:535` held the seed values. A warning naming "13.10.2026" for a
Bescheid that says 12.10. would have been a confident wrong date, so the
window is fixed in the same ticket (§9.11).

### What changed

- `@ds/domain` `courseEndingSoon(window, now)` — still offered, and the last day
  is within `ENDING_SOON_DAYS` (90) Berlin calendar days. Asked of the same
  window as `courseAvailability`, so the warning and the refusal cannot name
  different days. `berlinDaysBetween`, `startOfBerlinDay`, `parseIsoDate` in
  `berlin.ts`.
- `windowFromDates` / `datesOfWindow` — a form's `YYYY-MM-DD` pair to the first
  instant of the first Berlin day and the last of the last, and back.
- Console, Fortbildungen list: the Sichtbarkeit cell reads _"Sichtbar — endet in
  N Tagen"_ with _"Anerkennung gültig bis TT.MM.JJJJ."_ beneath.
- Console, course screen: a warning above every tab naming the last day, what
  happens after it, who applies for the extension (the Veranstalter — S14), and
  where the new date is entered.
- Console date fields store Berlin day boundaries and show the Berlin day.
- Seeds store `windowFromDates(...)`.
- Migration `0056` re-reads every value in exactly the two old shapes as the
  day its writer typed. Other times of day are untouched; it is idempotent.

- [x] "bis 12.10.2026" entered in the console keeps the course offered until

- [ ] Deployed, and the MEDICE course's row reads `2026-10-12T21:59:59.999Z`.

### Not done, deliberately

- **No email or alert.** The warning is on the screen an operator already
  uses. Pushing it to `ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL` is possible and is a separate
  decision.
- **Nothing for the learner.** A physician cannot renew an accreditation; telling
  them a course ends is a client decision about wording, not this ticket.
- **The renewal itself.** MEDICE files it with the ÄKWL (S14, closed on our
  side 27.08). The platform only makes sure somebody sees the date.
