# P136 — Every section says what it is for

**Status:** built. Copy plus one check, no new screens.

---

## The feedback

> _"Fortbildung UI is mostly self explanatory based on the Screens, so that part
> is clear. In the system, few parts are clear — as you are developing this as a
> product, I am looking at this from a developer point of view, so everything is
> clear, but if I look at the viewer who is going to use it, then few things
> still need to be improved. Maybe few more content explaining what each section
> does."_

The right read of this is not "add help text". It is that the console was
written by people who already know which of two similarly named screens they
want, and its copy answers _"what is in this table"_ where a newcomer is asking
_"which of these am I looking at, and what happens if I do nothing here"_.

## What was actually wrong

**`Fortbildungen` had no description at all** — the screen an operator opens
first, and the one where the important fact is invisible: a course they create is
a **draft** and stays unseen until they publish it. The screen was correct,
loaded for every role, and said nothing.

**Two pairs of screens whose names do not separate them.** Nothing on either
screen distinguished:

- **Zugänge** — a person who can sign in, one row per person — from
  **Teilnehmende** — that person's progress through one Fortbildung, one row per
  _enrolment_, so somebody taking two courses appears twice.
- **Bescheinigungen** — the document the physician receives — from
  **Punktemeldungen** — the statutory report to the Ärztekammer. They are
  separate screens because they succeed and fail independently, and nothing said
  so.

**`Erscheinungsbild` promises more than it holds.** The menu entry says
"appearance"; the screen is one font upload. Colours, logo and the
privacy-policy link are a **project's** branding and live under Organisation,
because they differ per surface and a typeface does not. An operator looking for
the brand colour found a font field and no statement that they were in the wrong
place — §9.4 with the reader standing directly in front of it.

## What changed

Six descriptions, in both locales. Each says what the section is, and where two
screens read alike, names the other one. The rule they follow: **the sentence
answers the question somebody has before they know the vocabulary**, not the one
they have after.

Everything already good was left alone — `Organisation`, `Mediathek`, `Texte`,
`Sicherheit`, `Konten` and `Kunden` already say what they are, and churn without
value is its own defect.

## The check

`scripts/role-matrix.mjs` already parses `NAV` for the role sweep, so the
assertion lives there: **every screen the console draws has a `description`.**
It runs in `pnpm verify` and in CI.

A missing sentence breaks nothing, which is exactly why it survived — there is no
failing request, no red test and no error. Now a screen added without one fails
the build, which is the only way a sentence nobody is forced to write gets
written.

Watched to go red by removing the `Fortbildungen` description again:

```
role-matrix: screens that do not say what they are for

  courses
```

## Not done
