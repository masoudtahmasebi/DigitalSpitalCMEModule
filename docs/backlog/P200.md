# P200 — The EIV connection test was on the other screen, and nothing said so

**Raised by:** the client, 07.09.2026 — _"i want to be able to test the eiv from
setting."_

---

## The feature exists

`EivCheckPanel` (P103-01) authenticates against EIV-FOBI with the VNR's password
and reads back the Veranstaltung the Ärztekammer holds for that number. It
reaches the two read-only capabilities and **never** `submit` — deliberately,
because a Punktemeldung cannot be unfiled, so a "test" button that could reach
`push_teilnahme` credits CME points to a real physician the first time somebody
clicks it to see what it does.

## So this is a §9.4 defect, not a missing feature

The panel is on the **course**, because the VNR and its password belong to the
accredited event rather than to the platform. The client was on
**Plattform → Punktemeldung**, which is where the register and the worker switch
live — the right place to ask the question and the wrong place to look, and it
said nothing either way.

The person who has just chosen the Echtsystem and armed the worker is exactly
the person who wants to know whether the credentials work, and they are standing
on that screen.

## The change

A short section on Plattform → Punktemeldung naming where the check is, and what
it does — including that it reports nobody, because "prüfen" against a register
that files statutory reports is a word somebody should hesitate over.

A sentence rather than a link: the console has no route from here to a
_particular_ course, and a link to the course list would land somebody one
screen short of the panel with nothing telling them what to open next. Naming
the path does the whole job.

## Not changed

The panel stays on the course. Moving it to the platform screen would mean
choosing a course there — a second course-picker, and a second place for the
VNR — which is §9.10b for the sake of one click.
