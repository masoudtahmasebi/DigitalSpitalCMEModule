# 08 · Mobile, keyboard and accessibility

**Assignee: Philipp Burka**
**Area:** Participant portal · **Duration:** approx. 30 minutes

## Goal

Check that the course is usable on a phone and without a mouse. Subtitles and
keyboard operability are **Level A** of the accessibility guidelines (WCAG,
EN 301 549) — without them, physicians with hearing or motor impairments cannot
complete the course.

## Prerequisites

- A real phone (not just a narrow browser window)
- A test course with a video **that has sound**

## Steps — mobile

1. Open the catalogue, detail page and player on the phone.
2. Watch the video in portrait, then rotate.
3. Open the module overview. Is it reachable on the phone at all?
4. Take a Lernerfolgskontrolle on the phone.
5. **Check sideways scrolling:** can the page be pushed left and right? It
   should not be.

## Steps — keyboard

6. Move down a page **using Tab only**. Is the focus always visible?
7. Start, pause and change the volume of the video **using the keyboard only**.
8. Open the keyboard-shortcut help (below the player). Are the entries correct?
9. Answer an exam question and submit, keyboard only.

## Steps — subtitles

10. Turn subtitles on. Do they appear?
11. If none are stored: is that said anywhere?

## Expected

- Step 5: **no sideways scrolling.** If the page wobbles or can be pushed,
  please report it with the screen width.
- Step 6: the focus is visible on **every** element.
- Steps 7 and 9: everything reachable by keyboard, with no mouse click.

## Pay particular attention to

Step 3. On wide screens the module overview sits to the right of the video — on
a phone it has to go somewhere else. If it is hard to find, that is a finding.

## Please report with

Screenshots from the phone (portrait and landscape) and the device model.
