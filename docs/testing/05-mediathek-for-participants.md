# 05 · Mediathek (materials for participants)

**Assignee: Philipp Burka**
**Area:** Participant portal · **Duration:** approx. 15 minutes

## Goal

Check that accompanying material is findable and that the locking logic stays
comprehensible.

## Prerequisites

- A test course with at least two materials (e.g. PDF), one of them tied to
  progress

## Steps

1. Open the **Mediathek** tab before watching anything.
2. Click a locked material. What happens, and what does it say?
3. Meet the condition (watch the video) and return.
4. Download a released material. Does it open correctly?
5. Check whether each material shows a **description**.
6. View it on a phone or in a narrow window.

## Expected

- Step 2: the lock names the **condition**, not just the state.
- Step 4: the download starts and the file is the expected one.
- Step 5: materials without a description are noticeable — please report if a
  card gets by with no explanatory text at all.

## Pay particular attention to

Can you tell from the card **what kind of file** it is (PDF, video, image) and
roughly how large? A physician between appointments decides on that basis
whether to click now.

## Please report with

A screenshot of the Mediathek grid, once wide and once narrow.
